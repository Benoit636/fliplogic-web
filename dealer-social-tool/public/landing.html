Questions dealers ask

Will it post things I didn’t approve?

Not unless you turn on full autopilot. In Assist mode (the default) every post waits in your approval queue.

Where do the vehicle details come from?

From your inventory — a CSV export from your DMS or an automatic feed URL. The AI only uses facts you provide.

Do I need a marketing person?

No. Once it’s set up, your weekly job is approving posts (or letting autopilot do it) and following up on the leads the bot flags.

Which networks are supported?

Facebook Pages, Instagram Business, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn Company Pages, X and TikTok.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription yourself from the Billing page. Your data stays available to export.

Is my data safe?

Each dealership’s data is isolated, social tokens are encrypted, and we never sell your data. See our privacy policy.
