Data deletion

To delete your dealership’s data (including data received from Facebook, Instagram, Google, LinkedIn, X or TikTok):

  1. Disconnect the account under Settings → Social accounts — this deletes its stored tokens immediately.
  2. Email [PRIVACY EMAIL] from an owner address asking to delete your dealership. We confirm and delete within 30 days.

You can also remove the Dealer Social app from your Facebook settings under Apps and Websites.
