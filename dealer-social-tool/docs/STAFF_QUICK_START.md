# Dealer Social: quick start for sales staff

**What it does:** turns a vehicle in our inventory into a ready-to-post Facebook / Instagram / LinkedIn post,
with a branded image and captions in English and French. A manager approves every post before it goes out.

## First time (2 minutes)

1. Open the invite link your manager sent you, enter your name and **choose your own password** (10+ characters).
   Never share it; everyone has their own login.
2. On iPhone: in Safari tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**. You now have a "DS" app icon.

## Make a post (about 1 minute)

1. **Create Post**, then type the **stock #** (or model) under *Or start from a vehicle in stock* and tap the vehicle.
2. Pick the goal: **Fresh Arrival**, **Featured Vehicle**, **Price Drop**, **Needs to Move**, **Manager Special** or **Trade-In Spotlight**.
3. Check the details. Add photos with **📷 Add photos** (take one or pick from your library). The first photo is the cover.
4. Tap **Create my post**. Check the image and the text:
   - **English / Français / EN + FR** changes the language of the captions and the image;
   - **Showcase / Bold / Clean** changes the look; **Square / Portrait / Story** the size.
5. Tap **Send for approval**. Done: your manager gets it in **Approvals**.

## After it's approved

**My Posts → Ready to post** → **Ready to post…**:

1. **Copy caption**
2. **Save image** (iPhone: choose **Save Image** to put it in Photos)
3. **Open Facebook** (or Instagram / LinkedIn), make the post with the saved image, paste the caption
4. Back in Dealer Social: **I posted it**

## Good to know

- Prices, payments and offers come only from what's in the inventory or what you type. The legal line is added automatically when a price is shown.
- A rejected post shows the manager's reason. Fix it in **My Posts → Rejected** and send it again.
- Photo refused as "HEIC"? Pick it again from the Photos picker, or set iPhone **Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible**.
- Forgot your password? Ask your manager to re-invite you (or use **Forgot your password?** if email is set up).
